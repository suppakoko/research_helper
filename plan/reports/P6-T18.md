## The tests, step by step

1. **English.**
   - The run: stored report → one script call (`AUDIO_SCRIPT_EN`) → 2 chunks → the model list, then one replayed
     `tts-en-short` body per chunk. Every request names a model from the recorded list, because `tts.model` is
     empty.
   - What was spoken: no Markdown, citation marker or link in any chunk.
   - The confirmations: both were asked, and the second carries the real chunks, their character count and a cost.
   - The WAV: its `RIFF` and `data` sizes agree with its length, and its data is exactly 2 × the recorded PCM plus
     one 250 ms gap. The run's `bytes` and `seconds` match.
   - The attachment: one import into the report's collection, as `audio/wav`, with no copy left on the disk.
   - The record: `audioScripts.en` and `audioAttachmentKeys.en` are stored, and the job record is `succeeded`.
2. **Korean from an English report.**
   - One `AUDIO_SCRIPT_KO` call, written from the English record with no companion in the store.
   - 2 Korean chunks, replayed from `tts-ko-short`, and a WAV of the right length.
   - `audioScripts.ko` and `audioAttachmentKeys.ko` are stored on the English record, and nothing is stored for
     English.
   - `sourceLanguage: "en"` is shown this way because the run's output does not carry it (finding 1).
3. **Failures.**
   - `error-invalid-key` gives `unentitled`.
     - The window gets `rh-error-tts` with `{ reason: "unentitled" }`, no stack, and no key in the failure.
     - The script is offered as text, and the offered save writes `scriptTextFileContent`.
     - The written report's sections and Markdown are unchanged, with no attachment key. Only the kept script was
       added.
     - The job record is `failed`.
   - `error-unknown-model` gives `model-gone`, and `no-audio-part` gives `no-audio`. Neither attaches anything.
4. **Cancel during chunk 2.**
   - The transport cancels the run when the second `:generateContent` arrives.
   - The outcome is `cancelled`: no audio held, nothing attached, nothing on the disk, no attachment key, and a
     `cancelled` job record.
   - The script is kept, and the next run uses it with no second script call.
   - A cancel offers no save. That is `P6-T14`'s rule, `offerScriptText: state.scriptAvailable && !cancelled`.
5. **Requests.** A whole English run is 1 + 2 requests, all answered by the replay on
   `generativelanguage.googleapis.com`. None reached a network.

## Findings

1. **The run's output does not carry `sourceLanguage` (not a card).** Step 2 asks for `sourceLanguage: "en"` "on the
   outcome". `SpokenScript.sourceLanguage` is set by `writeScript`, but `AudioReportOutput.script` carries only the
   language and the text, and the store keeps only the text. The test shows the same fact another way: the Korean
   prompt, the English record, no companion. Adding the field means changing `audioReportPipeline.ts`, which is
   outside this card's Files row. It does not block a Phase 6 finishing condition, so this stays a finding.
   Suggested, not adopted: add `sourceLanguage` to `AudioReportOutput.script` when `P6-T19`'s Korean briefings need
   to show it.
2. **Every fixture used is a stand-in.** This is the card's own open condition (`P6-T04`), not a new finding.
