## Coordinator-level decisions

All are recorded as "DECIDED 2026-11-05 (coordinator, under `plan/00` §6 item 313)" in `docs/05` §7.4's note.

1. **Word-space similarity and novelty:** similarity reads the collection's mean word vector and novelty reads the nearest member, so they stay two terms. This is backed by the measurement below.
2. **Space:** the profile decides. Centroids mean SPECTER2 (the Semantic Scholar paper vectors) and none means the word space. A keyed run with no vectors scores in words and says so.
3. **Missing evidence:** no vector or no text reads 0 on similarity and on novelty. It never falls back to words on SPECTER2.
4. **Fuzzy title:** a `"match"` verdict from `matchAgainstLibrary`. `"link"` and `"flag"` do not count as ownership.
5. **`contentFilters` reruns the identifier rules,** because the metadata fill-in can bring an identifier, date or type.
6. **`maxPerAuthor`** counts first and last authors only.
7. **Date window:** older than the first year is dropped; dated ahead and undated stay.
8. **Preprint** is `isPreprintWork`, because `10.1101/` is also Cold Spring Harbor's journals. "Published version owned" is a lookup, for preprints only.
9. **Reasons:** phrases first, then the two strongest terms by weight times value. `authorAffinity` is never offered. `novelty` is offered only above the default slider position. The fallback is `similarity`.
10. **Row type:** a composition, so `recommendation` is exactly what `put` takes.
11. **`topicIndex`** can be absent at k > 1 when a row has no vector and no topic group.
12. **MMR (maximal marginal relevance):** reads `cosine` as the run's space returns it. A row with no vector pays the full penalty after the first pick.
13. **Smaller rules:** ties, ORCID, ISSN, venue names and the rest are listed there.
14. **The yield** is every 5,000 comparisons, a starting value.
15. **The fixture run's figures** are recorded there.
