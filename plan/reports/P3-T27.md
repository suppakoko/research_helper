CONTRADICTIONS/FINDINGS
1. `screening.retractionPolicy` `include` (docs/07 §8.5) conflicts with docs/06 §13.3 ("must never contribute a finding") and this card's Do NOT.
   - Built: include summarizes the item for its per-paper note but never synthesizes it.
   - Recorded in docs/06 as open for the owner: either keep this reading or drop the value.
2. docs/06 §13.2 spells the policies exclude / translate_summary / include_untranslated; docs/07 §8.5 pins skip / translate_summary / summarize_in_source_language. docs/07 wins, and the mapping is recorded in docs/06.
3. Screening spends money during a search, but nothing estimates or confirms that spend.
   - The search pipeline's estimate() still reports no LLM cost, and there is no cost dialog in the search window.
   - The safeguards are: the per-run checkbox, which starts unticked; the budget guard's ceilings on every call; and the privacy check.
   - The owner may want P3-T16's confirmation in the search window.
4. Three docs/12 §3 behaviours are not built:
   - `isPrimaryResearch: false` items are "imported but tagged". The flag is recorded on the output; the tag needs the importer and writer, outside the Files list.
   - "Every score recorded" holds for the run output and the window's rows. Scores are not written into the FR-8 provenance note or the search_provenance row, because core/provenance.ts is outside the Files list.
   - `{{INTERPRETATION}}` is `none`, because QUERY_EXPAND does not exist.
5. Crossref `updated-by` and PubMed publication types are parsed only from a response the caller passes. Today no caller holds one: the mappers drop both fields and SourceRecord.raw is not kept. Layers (b) and (c) are dormant until P3-T29, or a mapper change, supplies them.
6. `readRetraction`'s reason reading assumes `getData` returns an object with a `reasons` string array. That shape is unverified: typings declare only `isRetracted`, and docs/06 gives line numbers, not types. Any other shape gives no reason; the item is still reported retracted.
7. Found while writing fixtures (P2-T13's area, not changed): dedupe.ts's fuzzy title tier calls two papers one work when their long titles differ by one inner number. The case found was "A synthetic study number 1 of widget counts in three regions" against the same title with "number 2", with the same author and year. The series-marker guard looks only at the title's end. In a collection, this would drop one paper as a duplicate.
8. OpenAI and OpenRouter send json_schema with `strict: true`. RelevanceScreen's optional `flags` and its `maxLength`, `minimum` and `maximum` may be refused by OpenAI's strict mode, which the PaperSummary schema already faces. Unverified, because no live call was made.
9. The card's Files list omitted the composition root, the pipeline's types.ts, the stylesheet, two tests that pin the window layout, and an integration spec's dependency list. All were edited minimally and are listed above.
10. The search window's run-level warnings for screening are English strings in `output.warnings`, the same pattern as the existing ones. They are logged, not displayed; what the user sees are the two MessageRefs from `screeningWarnings`.
