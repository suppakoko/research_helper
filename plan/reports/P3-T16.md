CONTRADICTIONS/FINDINGS
1. The job runner records a budget refusal as a failed item. `runPooledItems` in src/core/jobQueue/queue.ts (P3-T17's file, outside my Files) does not pause on it. For the product to pause mid-run with the item kept pending, P3-T29 or a small runner change must:
   - catch it with `ceilingStopOf`;
   - request `pause("budget-stop")`;
   - re-dispatch that item on resume.
   Without it, a mid-run ceiling stops only that item.
2. Wiring left for P3-T35 and P3-T29:
   - pass `getSessionSpendCounter()` (src/bootstrap/registerUI.ts) to every job's `createBudgetGuard`, replacing `UNWIRED_BUDGET_GUARD` in createPipelineContextFactory;
   - wrap the router with `withBudget(router, { pricesFor, now })`, where pricesFor comes from the model catalogue;
   - pass `budget: ctx.budget` in each LLMCallContext;
   - install `createConfirmResumeGuard` and `createCeilingsResumeGuard` into ResumeGuards (they need P3-T29's remaining-estimate function);
   - pass `confirmRunCost` to the summarize pipeline.
   The session counter lives in registerUI (module scope) because container.ts is shared. P3-T35 may move it into the service graph.
3. A raise ("continue") lives only in the guard. After a restart, the ceilings resume guard asks again. `JobRecord.budgetUsd` is unused.
4. Choices for the owner to confirm (all recorded in the docs/07 §4.3 note):
   - "the stricter binds" means the least room left;
   - headroom is 1.15×, not 20 %;
   - output is reserved at the request's `maxOutputTokens` ceiling, and a request without `maxOutputTokens` reserves no output, so P3-T25 must always send it;
   - an unpriced model's reservation passes the ceilings, its tokens are counted, and the dialog requires the second acknowledgement.
