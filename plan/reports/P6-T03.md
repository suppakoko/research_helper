## Tests added (`test/unit/scripts/recordFixturesRedaction.test.ts`, 54 in the file)

The tests use a synthetic Gemini key assembled at run time and a fake Gemini transport. They cover:

- **Dry run.** It lists the seven scenarios and the estimate, and reads no key. The script scenarios are reported
  pending while their prompts are absent.
- **Pricing.** Only the two billed sentences are priced, from `docs/04` §4.2's table and the 30-second bound, and

- **Command line.** `gemini-tts` is accepted, and its options are allowed only for an LLM provider or

- **The gates.** The run stops without `--live` or without the key (exit 2), at the spend gate (exit 3), and when
  the cap is below the estimate; nothing is sent in any of these.
- **Unlisted model.** No billed call is made when the model is missing from the list.
- **A full fake recording:**
  - The key appears only in the header, and the altered key is sent exactly once.
  - Five fixtures are written, and none contains `x-goog-api-key`, `key=` or `Bearer`.
  - The audio is preserved byte for byte, the model list is filtered, and the sidecars are correct.
- **Refusals.** An answer that still holds `key=` after redaction refuses the whole run, and nothing is written. A
  body over 300 KB (a 5-second tone) is refused.
- **`assertCleanTtsFixture`.** It refuses the key header's name, `key=` and the key's value, and lets the audio
  through.
- **Synthetic mode.** It writes every stand-in and never writes over a recording. That covers a sidecar with

- **Committed stand-ins.** They are clean and under 300 KB, and the audio decodes to 0.4 seconds of 24 kHz 16-bit
  PCM.
- **Script scenarios:**
  - They are reached only through `--only`, and they are pending or ready according to the prompt file.
  - Their stand-ins hold no marker, and their lengths are within ±30 % of one minute.
  - Synthetic mode writes them for OpenRouter only, and never over a recording.

## Decisions taken (each DECIDED 2026-11-16, coordinator, under `plan/00` §6 item 313)

1. **The TTS fixtures are keyed by scenario name**, `<scenario>.json` and `<scenario>_meta.json`, as the LLM
   fixtures are (`llmFixtureKey`). This keeps one naming rule for every keyed source.
2. **`models-list` is fetched unpaged and kept to its TTS entries.** `nextPageToken` is dropped, with a note in the
   sidecar. The fixture's purpose is the TTS entries (`docs/04` §2.2), and the full list would be large and change
   often.
3. **The script scenarios are kept out of `LLM_SCENARIOS`** and recorded only when `--only` names them. A provider's
   default recording and spend therefore do not change, and the eleven-row LLM contract suite stays as it was.
   While their prompt files are absent the scenarios are "pending", and they become ready when `P6-T06` lands, with
   no further edit. *(Amended 2026-11-16, later, during `P6-T06`: "ready" now means the file loads through the
   registry's parser, not merely that it exists. `audioScriptPromptPath` became `audioScriptPrompt`, which returns
   the template or the reason it is pending. The files are held verbatim in `src/prompts/pending/`, because they do
   not load yet; see `plan/reports/P6-T06.md`.)*
4. **The script stand-ins are written for OpenRouter only**, the default `llmProvider`. The owner's `reportModel`
   provider may differ, and the owner's run writes there.
5. **Each billed TTS call is priced at 30 seconds of audio.** That is a ceiling about ten times the three-second
   target, so the estimate is a true upper bound.
6. **A synthetic run never writes over a recording.** A file counts as a stand-in only if its sidecar says
   `"synthetic": true`, and a fixture with no sidecar counts as a recording.
7. **`test/contract/llm/_llmContract.ts` was edited, although it is outside `Files`.** The script stand-ins sit in
   `test/fixtures/llm/openrouter/` (step 2 puts them on the LLM path), and the suite's "pairs every fixture with a
   sidecar" test failed on them. The edit is the smallest one that lets the suite ignore files that are not its
   scenarios.
8. **`key=` is refused anywhere in a TTS fixture or sidecar**, not only in a URL. The key never travels in a URL on
   this path, so any `key=` is a leak or a mistake.
9. **The dropped key header is named "[the key header]" in sidecars.** Otherwise the sidecar would fail the
   card's own "no `x-goog-api-key`" rule.
