## CONTRADICTIONS/FINDINGS
1. **The cap the estimate assumed is respected, with one gap: calibration.** The 140 is guaranteed by raw `estTokens` with no model id, measured as sent (after escaping), which is what `planSynthesis` assumes. If P4-T05 step 1 measures the joined digests with `estTokens(…, modelId)`, a model whose calibration factor is above 1 counts a digest above 140. The estimate's 1.15 headroom covers factors only up to 1.15. P4-T05 should measure the same way, or use `digestTokens`.
2. **The docs/06 §8.2 example itself is 169 tokens by `estTokens`, above the "~90–140" band.** Fixed truncation cuts it to 126 (the Q, Methods, Findings and Keywords lines are cut). "200 digests ≈ 24 k" still holds: at most 28 k at the cap.
3. **The grounding warning does apply to digests.** Findings and metrics are withheld on `confidence` `low` or `none`, or on a row carrying `grounding_low_findings_excluded`.
   - P3-T25 always sets `low` together with the warning; the warning check is a safeguard in case a row ever has one without the other.
   - Treating `none` like `low` is my reading; the card only says "low-confidence".
   - The card also withholds Metrics, which docs/06 §12.1 L3 does not mention; built as the card says.
4. **Who calls `toDigest`.** The card's Files row names `cluster.ts`, `compose.ts` and `scaffold.ts` as callers. But `toDigest` needs the item's year, which only the run has, and P4-T18 step 1 lists "digest" as its own stage. My reading: P4-T18 builds the digests and P4-T05 consumes `digestText`. The coordinator may want to align the card's Files wording.
5. **Bad year or key input throws a RangeError.** `toDigest` throws when the year is not a whole number 0–9999 (`undefined` prints `n.d.`), or when `content.itemKey` does not match `^[A-Z0-9]{8}$`. P4-T18 must parse years before calling.
6. **Stale "NOT BUILT" text in plan/07.** The card's Notes still say P3-T25 is "NOT BUILT"; my dated note says that no longer holds. plan/07 §0's entry-condition rows (the "Phase 3 DONE, a real StoredSummary corpus" row and the P3-T25 row) also still read Not met / NOT BUILT. Those are outside my scope and were not edited.
7. **Heavy import for one constant.** `digest.ts` imports `GROUNDING_LOW_WARNING` from `summarizeItem.ts`, which pulls the summarizer module into the trend report's import graph just for one string. Moving the constant to `src/model/summary.ts` would be cleaner. That file is outside my Files list.
8. **Step 3 needed no code.** `renderPrompt` already passes every variable through `escapeSourceText`. The test confirms `<<<` stays unescaped in the digest and is gone after escaping.
