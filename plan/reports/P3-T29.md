3. **P3-T28's `generate` and `saveAsNote` are unbound.** `writeSummaryNote` now exists for `saveAsNote`. Who runs a one-item summary is still undecided.
4. **P3-T25 finding 9 (warnings lost on a cache hit) is not fixed.** It needs `summaryCache.ts`, which is outside this card.
5. **No automatic resumption after a restart.** The notifier exists now, but `remaining` and the `skipCached` probe are not supplied, and the pipeline declares `resumable: false`. An interrupted summarize job is failed at the next start. The resumed notice is installed but never fires.
6. **Choices the documents left open, all recorded for the owner to confirm:**
   - The key check pauses on any tier, not only the session tier.
   - The outage rule waits for at least 10 settled items.
   - "Fell back to abstract" means summarized from the abstract alone.
   - The cache probe counts an item as cached only on an exact hash match.
   - Removal moves notes to the trash; it erases nothing.
   - Re-summarize keeps the existing note; no "regenerate / keep mine" choice is built.
   - The model tag format is `research_helper/model:<provider>/<model>`.
   - `summary.fullTextMode` is not a per-run control in the window.
7. **Not wired:** the §13.1 abstract recovery, because no consent surface exists. Europe PMC Tier 3 network fetches during acquisition are product behaviour and were not exercised.
8. **`PipelineSet.summarize` is optional,** so `searchImport.test.ts` did not need editing. That loses the compile-time "missing member" guarantee.
