## CONTRADICTIONS/FINDINGS
1. **The estimate quotes much higher than `docs/06` §5.4's worked example.** Because each synthesis call's input is priced at its §5.2 budget:
   - The 42-paper example (K=5) comes to $4.68 against the example's ~$2.90, of which $2.08 is synthesis against roughly $0.41 at typical fill.
   - With K unset (7 themes) it is $5.06. With every summary already made it is $2.08 (K=5) or $2.46 (K=7).
   - The pricing never under-states, but routine report runs will usually pass the $2.00 threshold.
   - A tighter bound that stays safe needs `P4-T04`'s digests and `P3-T25`'s stored summaries, which do not exist yet. Owner's call whether to tighten it later.
2. **Calls the estimate does not reserve.** Calls that follow a malformed answer, the one fresh generation for a missing heading (`P4-T08`), and `P4-T09`'s per-section critique calls are not reserved. The belief in `P4-T09`'s Notes is confirmed by `withBudget`'s header: each request reserves before it is sent, so a ceiling pauses the job and asks. Not counting the regeneration matches `estimateRelevanceScreen`'s precedent and the card's Done-when count (1+5+1+1 plus one rewrite).
3. **Possible under-statement on themes.** `P4-T05`'s card does not reject a clustering answer with more themes than `MAX_THEMES`. If the model returns more, the theme stage makes more calls than were reserved. Suggest `P4-T05` caps themes at `MAX_THEMES`. Until then, `withBudget` is the backstop.
4. **`docs/06` §9.2 has no row below 10 or above 400 papers.** I used the first row and the last row (recorded in the note). `themeCountBandFor` is exported, so `P4-T05` can reuse it instead of transcribing the table a second time.
5. **Choice for the owner: the report notice does not force the confirmation on its own.** So a collection's stored "don't ask again" record (which may have come from a summarize run) can skip a report run's routine question when the cost is below the threshold and no ceiling is hit. Making the notice force the question would need a new field in `RoutineConfirmation` / `collection_settings`, which is outside this card's files.
6. **Two-stage batch ceiling.** A 60-paper batch's ceiling is 4 400 tokens, which could exceed a model whose output limit is below that. The estimate still plans batches of 60; this is a `P4-T06` concern.
7. **What P4-T18 must pass in.** The caller must supply:
   - the prompt registry;
   - each synthesis prompt's own token count, measured with the calibrated `estTokens` over the template;
   - `reportModel.maxOutputTokens` from `ModelInfo`;
   - the three models with their prices;
   - the `report` input to `buildEgressDisclosure`.
