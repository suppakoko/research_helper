     - a finding or pattern that lost all its keys is dropped, and so is a contradiction with an emptied side;

CONTRADICTIONS/FINDINGS
1. **Open for the owner: depth 2 has a ceiling.**
   - One merge is only sure to carry 11 partials at the 3 000-token ceiling × 1.15, which is about 231 summaries of 1 840 tokens.
   - A theme needing more sub-batches is reported unanalysed before any spend.
   - The alternative: send the sub-batches and fail only on measured overflow. That keeps themes that would have fitted, but pays for sub-batches that may be thrown away.
   - This is plausible at 400 papers with one dominant theme.
2. **Open for the owner and P4-T18: what happens when every theme is unanalysed.** No document says. Nothing was built for it.
3. **Followup-307 finding 1, with numbers.** The estimate assumes 1 840 tokens per summary (1 600 × 1.15) and 38 400 ÷ 1 840 ≈ 20.9 summaries per call across themes. My packing fits 21 per call within one theme. So a theme of n > 21 such summaries takes ⌈n/21⌉ calls plus one merge.
   - Themes that each fit one call stay within the reservation (K or K + 1 calls).
   - followup-307's 150-paper mix (six themes of 22, three of 6) needs 15 CLUSTER_REDUCE + 6 merges = 21 calls; `planSynthesis` reserves 10 + 0.
   - 400 papers in twelve themes of 33 plus one of 4 need 25 + 12 = 37 calls, against 20 + 8 = 28 reserved.
   - Both comparisons are pinned in tests. `withBudget` reserves the extra calls at dispatch. Real summaries are usually shorter (900–1 600 per docs/06 §8.1), which fits more per call. The estimate was not changed.
4. **Two readings the documents left open:**
   - Effect sizes are withheld together with `keyFindings`, matching P4-T04's digest. docs/06 §12.1 names only `keyFindings`.
   - A sub-batch is checked against its own members, which is stricter than "the theme's member set".
5. **`passes` versus P4-T18's Done-when.** `passes` counts levels (1 or 2), consistent with P4-T05. P4-T18's "pass count equals the calls made at L1–L4" still conflicts with this (P4-T05 finding 10).
6. **docs/06 §8.5's pseudocode** still calls CLUSTER_REDUCE for the merge; P4-T09 owns §8.5.
7. **Small duplication:** `sumUsage` is now a third private copy (also in cluster.ts and jsonMode.ts).
