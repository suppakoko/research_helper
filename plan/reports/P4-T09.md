## COORDINATOR-LEVEL DECISIONS YOU TOOK
All of these are recorded as DECIDED 2026-10-30 (coordinator, under item 313) in docs/06 §12.2's "As built" note.
1. **What is critiqued.** Only §1–§10 of the draft, each under its heading. The preamble and the model's §11 are not sent, because the scaffold drops the first and replaces the second.
2. **The cited set and the evidence.**
   - The cited set is `bibliographyKeys` (§1–§10, first appearance first).
   - Each summary is sent as the theme stage sends it (`summariesForReduce`), so findings and effect sizes are withheld for low-confidence summaries.
3. **The budget.** The 60 000 tokens cover the draft text, the key list, and each summary's JSON plus one separator token, less one for the brackets. This is P4-T07's packing rule. The draft goes in one call when `packByTokens` returns one batch.
4. **`{{VALID_ITEM_KEYS}}`** is the keys whose summaries that call carries.
5. **Per section.**
   - One call per non-empty section, run in parallel under the pool.
   - A section too large on its own is cut into runs of paragraphs that each fit.
   - A paragraph whose own cited summaries cannot fit is not critiqued. That gives the warning `critique_text_unchecked`.
   - Problems are combined in section order. A problem with no section gets its section's heading. The combined verdict is the worst one.
6. **The verdict decides nothing.** Any `high` problem triggers a rewrite; a round with no `high` problem ends the loop.
7. **`unresolved`** is every problem of the last critique, of every severity. Nothing is dropped.
8. **Settings.** Rounds and the critique model are read when the stage runs. `critiqueRoundsOf` and `resolveCritiqueModel` are exported so P4-T18's `rp` key and estimate read them the same way.
9. **The signal for P4-T20** is `onRevision`; a callback that throws is logged and ignored.
