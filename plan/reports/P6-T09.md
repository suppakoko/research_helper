## Decisions taken (each DECIDED 2026-11-16, coordinator, under `plan/00` §6 item 313; recorded in the file header)

1. **Chunks are exact slices of the script.** Each separator is the exact text up to the next chunk, and the
   first chunk starts at offset 0. This is what lets the join reproduce the script.
2. **The upper target is a hard ceiling.** The sketch's `minChars` is dropped.
3. **Where a sentence ends.** A sentence ends at a terminal mark that is followed by whitespace or by the end of
   the text. Closing quotes or brackets may come in between.
4. **How an over-long sentence is split.** It is cut after the last comma or semicolon that is followed by
   whitespace. Failing that, it is cut at the last space outside a guarded abbreviation, and failing that, at the
   ceiling. A cut never falls inside a surrogate pair. Each forced split is logged by its kind, position and
   length, never by its text.
5. **Korean sentence ends are `.`, `?` and `!`.** The abbreviation guard (`ENGLISH_ABBREVIATIONS`) applies to
   English only.
6. **A paragraph gap is a blank line,** the pattern `/\s*\n[^\S\n]*\n\s*/`.
