## Decisions (coordinator, under item 313)

1. **The table lives in `src/llm/shared/`**, as the card says, and reaches `ui/` as data (above).
2. **One line per provider, and two for Gemini**, because its paid and free tiers make opposite training claims.
3. **One date for every row**, `POLICY_NOTES_AS_OF`, because every page was read the same day; each row still has its
   own `asOf`, so a later partial re-verification can date rows separately.
4. **A correction is recorded, not shown.** `docs/09` §3.3's Gemini paid row said retention is "configurable to
   7/14/28/55 days in AI Studio"; the logs policy says that window belongs to the Gemini API logs a project keeps,
   separate from the 55-day abuse-monitoring retention. The correction is a dated note under the re-verification
   table in `docs/09` §3.3, and the pane does not mention the window.
5. **OpenRouter's routed providers** are covered by one sentence saying each has its own policy, which the privacy
   page states.
