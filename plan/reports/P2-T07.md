## Contradictions with the card or corpus

1. **Card step 3, Done-when 3: the planner half sits outside Files, and the wording would narrow Europe PMC.** The owners are `queryFor` (`stages.ts`) and `explainEuropePmcQuery` / `buildEuropePmcSearchRequest` (`src/sources/europepmc/query.ts`). Europe PMC's default search already includes `PPR` records. Adding `AND SRC:PPR` to the one Europe PMC query, as the step reads, would restrict Europe PMC to preprints and drop its journal results. A decision is needed first: a second Europe PMC query, an OR clause, or no change because preprints are already included.
2. **`docs/02` §8.5, "check `messages[0].status === "ok"`", is not enough.** The not-found answers measured on 2026-10-15 are not `"ok"` and not errors:
   - `/details/` answers `"no posts found"`.
   - `/pubs/` answers `"no articles found for published version of "`; the DOI is missing for a `10.64898/` DOI and present for `10.1101/459891`.
   - Applied literally, the rule would turn every unknown DOI into an error. `parseBiorxivBody` has three outcomes instead, using `BIORXIV_NOT_FOUND_STATUSES`.
3. **The recorder's redaction mangled a schema key.** `redact()`'s 40-character catch-all scrubbed the `/pubs/` member name `preprint_author_corresponding_institution` (41 characters) to `"[redacted:41]"` in the first recording of both `record-preprint` scenarios.
   - Fixed by exempting that one name through `opaqueTokens` (`BIORXIV_LONG_MEMBER_NAMES`), then re-recording 3 scenarios.
   - `docs/13` §3.2 says the exemption is for "pagination tokens by name … and nothing else". A member name is outside that letter.
   - The sidecar note written by `redactExchange` reads "preserved 1 opaque pagination token(s)", which is inaccurate wording for this case.
4. **The host does not answer across servers** (measured): a medRxiv DOI asked of `/details/biorxiv/` returns `no posts found`. Since the `10.64898/` prefix cannot name the server (`docs/02` §11.2), a caller holding an unattributed DOI must ask both adapters. `docs/02` §8 does not record this.
5. **§8.5's flattening tokens were not seen; run-in headings were seen in `/details/` too.** None of the 7 recorded abstracts contains `O_SCPCAP` or `C_LIO_LI`. §8.5 places run-in headings in `/pubs/`, but they also appear in medRxiv `/details/` abstracts. §8.5 says "strip"; the mapper turns a heading into a `"Heading: "` prefix, the convention PubMed and Europe PMC already use. The token handling is tested only on hand-built text.
6. **`docs/13` §3.1 set "for each" server.** These rows have nothing to record on a lookup-only host:
   - `search-typical`, `search-single` and `paginated`: there is no keyword search.
   - `dedup-pair-set`: per `plan/00` item 168.
   - `record-jats-abstract`: first planned as `/pubs/` for the old-style `10.1101/459891`, which was not found even though that paper is published in *Cell*. The scenario was renamed `pubs-old-style-doi`.
   - medRxiv also has no `record-unicode`.
7. **Licence values**: measured `cc_by_nc` and `cc_no`, neither in §8.5's list.
8. **Fields with no slot or no presence**:

   - No recorded record carries the `funder` member that §8.3's sample shows.
