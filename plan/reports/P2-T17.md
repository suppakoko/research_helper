## 6. Contradictions with the card or the corpus

1. **Import runs record no sources (a FR-8 defect found while building this card).** When the window imports, the pipeline is handed the records it already fetched. In that case the planning step asks no source and leaves `perSource` empty; `P2-T31`'s report records the same fact. So every record the window had ever written had `perSource: []`. That means no per-source section in the note, and nothing for a re-run to read its sources and limit from.
   - Fix, inside the view model: the search's per-source reports are held (`previewedReports`) and used when the import run reports none.
   - Covered by the unit test "records the search's sources on the import's row…" (killed M16) and by the integration test's check of `perSource`.
   - **Effect on existing notes:** notes written from now on show a per-source section. Earlier notes and rows do not.
2. **Separator placement (step 5).** Step 5 says the entry goes "below the separator", but the submenu has no separator. In `docs/08` §2.5 the separator divides the two AI jobs (report, summarize) from the entries that open the search window. Neither AI job is registered yet, and the existing "Search & Import into This Collection…" entry belongs to the window-opening group. So no separator was added. The reason is recorded in the `registerMenus.ts` header.
3. **Synchronous `hasRunForCollection` (`docs/08` §2.4).** §2.4 writes it as a synchronous call, but a SQLite read is asynchronous. Measured in Zotero 10.0.3's `menuManager.js`:
   - `onShowing` is not awaited;
   - submenu entries are built when the submenu itself opens;
   - `setVisible` works on the live element through a weak reference, so a later call still reaches the open menu.

   Solution: `ProvenanceIndex` keeps cached answers. The parent submenu starts the lookup early, and a late answer is applied only if the same single collection is still selected.
4. **What "already present" counts.** `docs/08` §4.6 and FR-12 say "already in this collection", but the importer can only report "matched an existing item in the library". Its `linkedExisting` count includes items that were outside the target collection, and its `skipped` count also includes a `workKey` repeated inside one run.
   - The summary therefore counts `linkedExisting + skipped`.
   - The banner text says "in your library" and notes that choosing "Import anyway" overrides the count.
5. **Two departures from the `docs/08` §4.6 drawing.**
   - The banner does not claim the client-side filters were pre-filled: the stored record (`docs/07` §5.3) has no field for them.
   - The window has no "All years" control, so a recorded `dateFilter: "none"` is shown with a new string, `research-helper-search-rerun-all-years`. In practice the window cannot create such a record anyway.
6. **Verify-with chain.** Its locale `diff` cannot pass while Korean is pending review (see section 1).
7. **Baseline unit count.** The unit count was 2,415 at the end. Of these, 61 tests are this card's; the rest of the rise from 2,312 comes from the agents working in parallel.
