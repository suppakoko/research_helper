## Coordinator-level decisions
Each is recorded as "DECIDED 2026-11-05 (coordinator, under `plan/00` §6 item 313)": items 1 to 10 in `docs/05` §7.2's note, item 11 in `docs/07` §5.2's note.

1. **Which space a run is on.** A run with at least one vector for a member is on SPECTER2. A run with none is lexical, with no centroids, labels or `embeddingModel`.
2. **What "gain" means.** A k must beat the k held so far by `minGain`. One topic counts as silhouette 0. When two k are close the smaller wins, because each topic costs one request.
3. **Spherical k-means.** It uses the highest cosine, a normalised mean and `1 − cosine` for the silhouette. It starts with k-means++ from a fixed seed, with 3 restarts and at most 50 passes, and members are sorted by work key first. Topics are numbered largest first.
4. **Sampling.** The fit and silhouette read at most 600 members, a seeded sample. Every member is then assigned, and `minClusterSize` is checked on all of them.
5. **The sketch's three undefined scores.**
   - Recency is §7.4's decay over whole years.
   - Quality is `min(1, ln(1 + citations) / ln(1 + 1000))`.
   - Centrality is the cosine with the member's own centroid, or on the lexical space the dot product with the mean word vector. It is stretched so the collection's lowest reads 0 and its highest 1.
6. **Seeds are taken in turn from each topic.** A cut alone would rarely stop one topic from taking all the seeds, and P5-T23 sends one request per topic. A member with no topic comes last.
7. **The lexical cut is 0.45,** measured as described under Findings.
8. **Floor and ceiling.** Below 10 seeds, skipped papers return in score order. The ceiling is `RECOMMEND_MAX_POSITIVES`. A work with no identifier `toSemanticScholarId` can use is never a seed.
9. **Labels.** A label is weight × (share of the topic's members holding the term − share of the other members holding it). It is the best term a larger topic has not used, and if any topic has none the whole field is left out.
10. **Negatives.** A store that cannot be read warns and answers none. Only a `ResearchHelperError` is caught.
11. **`embeddingModel` and `embeddingDim`** are present only when centroids exist.

## Findings (none blocks a finishing condition; no new card proposed)
**1. Without a key, seeds do not spread across topics.** The lexical cut was measured on recorded PubMed abstracts: different papers reach at most 0.361 (10 011 pairs across 142 abstracts). A synthetic second version of one paper reads at least 0.494 with half its abstract's words replaced, and at least 0.515 with no abstract on one side. So 0.45 stops versions of one paper and nothing else. Ranked by year alone, the 15 seeds of those 142 abstracts all came from one file at every cut from 0.15 to 0.92. The built `selectSeeds` did the same on the 156 abstracts of the seven files holding at least 14: 15 of 15 from one file. One file stands in for one topic here. This narrows generators (b) and (c) on a keyless run of a multi-topic collection, and generator (a) does not run without a key. No finishing condition asks for spread on that path, so it can wait for P5-T30's verdict or `plan/00` §6.

**2. `minGain` decides k once silhouettes come near it.** On synthetic vectors at 768 values, a single topic stayed k = 1 on every run, with a highest silhouette of 0.012. Clear topics were found at low noise. At noise 3.0, four true topics gave k = 4 on 3 of 10 runs and k = 1 on 7. How high real SPECTER2 silhouettes run is unknown, so 0.08 could collapse real multi-topic collections to one topic. P5-T30's note records what to read.

**3. A partly failed vector fetch can be stored as fresh.** If `ensureEmbeddings` returns `failure` or a non-empty `notAsked`, centroids come from only some members, and `membershipHash` and `embeddingModel` still match on later runs. P5-T25 should not treat such a profile as settled. This card only handles the case of no vectors at all.

**4. The 0.15 quality weight may often do nothing.** `citationCount` on a collection member appears to come from the item's `Citations:` line in Extra, according to a grep of `itemMapper.ts`. Items without that line would score 0 on this term. This was not measured on a real library.

**5. Labels can go stale.** A term the user removes stays on a label until the next rebuild, because the labels are built before `userEdits` apply. P5-T27 can decide whether that matters.
