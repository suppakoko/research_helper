How the pool size relates to the per-host cap of 3
- The pool's size is `concurrency`, clamped to 1–8. It is re-read before every permit is taken, so a change in the pane applies at the next dispatch, and a smaller size drains through `Semaphore.resize`.
- The pool bounds this plugin's LLM work across all jobs. §7.3's LLM rows (item 260) separately allow at most 3 requests in flight to any one host.
- So with `concurrency` at 3 or below, the pool is the binding limit. Above 3, the extra permit holders wait inside that host's `TokenBucket.run`, cancellably.
- Measured in the test with `concurrency` 8 over the `api.openai.com` row: 8 calls held pool permits at once, and at most 3 reached the host.

The pinned-tags hook: supplied
- `createRunningWork()` is built in `installServices`. Its `pinnedTags` goes to the cache through `installPluginDatabase`, and the same registry goes to `buildPipelineSet`.
- `runPooledItems` holds `work:<key>` for every item of a live run, completed items included (a paused or re-run job needs them as cache hits), and releases them when the run ends.
- No pipeline calls the runner yet. P3-T29 is the caller (rule 10), and until then the registry is always empty.

4. §7.5 says a pause releases the token bucket's queued waiters "and re-queues them on resume". The runner cannot tell a call waiting in a host bucket from one already sending. Such a call counts as in flight and is sent, waiting at most about (pool − 1) seconds at 1 request per second. Recorded in `docs/07` as not built; it needs the HTTP layer.
5. Cancellation from the job's own token (`ctx.token`, for example a shutdown) stops the run but moves no job state, leaving it for `recover()` / `drain()`. Only `PooledRun.cancel` moves `running → cancelling → cancelled`. The runner never moves a job to `succeeded` or `failed`; that belongs to the pipeline after its later stages.
6. The card's "reports 'cancelled at 87/200'" is met by `PooledRunOutcome.stoppedAt` / `total` and by `describePooledOutcome`, which is developer-facing and goes into the log line "llm pool run stopped". No user-visible string, so no en-US or ko-KR entries were added.
