## CONTRADICTIONS/FINDINGS
1. **Step 3's open line is not ruled** (plan/04 §0 rule 10); details are in the verdict. The corpus splits cleanly: six texts are at most 0.191 and 34 at least 0.664.
2. **The pre-run estimate can under-quote section-aware papers.** P3-T16's `fullTextCalls` in `src/pipeline/shared/estimate.ts` counts chunked papers as ⌈(T−250)/5750⌉ `CHUNK_MAP` calls. On the section-aware path every section starts its own chunk, so there are more calls. On a synthetic 30,830-token paper: section-aware makes 12 chunks against 6 flat. That breaks the rule that the estimate "may over-quote, never under-quote". In the other direction, the estimate has no 13-chunk ceiling, so it over-quotes very long papers. This is for the P3-T25/P3-T29 owner or a plan/00 §6 item.
3. **Section-aware sends less than flat under pressure.** On the synthetic 60,938-token paper: 85% against 100% at full budget, and 47% against 60% at the halved budget. This bears on the G-22 default. Also, 39 of the 40 corpus texts are single-shot at default budgets, so on that corpus the path choice reaches one paper (O3).
4. **`CHUNK_REDUCE` expects document order.** Its user text says "CHUNK EXTRACTIONS (JSON array, in document order)", but section-aware chunks come in synthesis order. Each chunk carries `start`, so P3-T25 should sort the map outputs by it before the reduce call.
5. **The §13.5 retry does not help a context-length error from `CHUNK_REDUCE`.** Halving the chunk budget makes more chunks and a larger reduce input. Not handled here; for P3-T25.
6. **Constants are duplicated across layers.** The budget constants exist both in chunking.ts and in `estimate.ts` (`MAP_CALL_BUDGETS`, `ESTIMATE_HEADROOM`, `CHUNK_OVERLAP_TOKENS`), because `llm/` may not import `pipeline/`. A test pins them equal. Later, estimate.ts could import them from chunking.ts.
