## What the screen compares now (Do step 1)

`candidatePairsOf` in `screen.ts` builds two kinds of group from cheap keys, then yields each pair once:

- **Exact groups.** Two items are compared if they share any of these:
  - an identifier (each of `ID_KINDS`: DOI, PubMed id, PubMed Central id, arXiv id, Semantic Scholar id);
  - a bioRxiv accession (the same preprint under the `10.1101/` and `10.64898/` prefixes);
  - a normalized title that is not empty.
- **Fuzzy groups**, for the cascade's fuzzy-title tier (Tier 6). Two items are compared only when all of these hold:
  - both titles are comparable and both years are known (each of the tier's three rules needs that);
  - both have the same key, "preprint or not" plus the title's numbers in order;
  - the years are at most `MAX_YEAR_DELTA` apart (the group is sorted by year and the loop stops at the window's end);
  - the same first author, or titles whose lengths allow a Levenshtein similarity of at least the tier's threshold.

**Why nothing the old screen found is lost.** `sameWork` accepts a "match", or a "link" or "flag" for a preprint pair
with equal titles. Each way to get one needs a shared exact key, or a fuzzy-tier match:

- **The key's "preprint or not" half.** `guardTitleVerdict` turns a fuzzy match into a flag when exactly one side is
  a preprint, and `sameWork` accepts such a flag only for equal titles, which the exact title group already compares.
- **The key's numbers half.** The guard also turns a fuzzy match into a flag when the titles' numbers differ.
- **The length bound.** A Levenshtein distance is never below the length difference.

The full argument is in the doc comment on `candidatePairsOf`.

`findCollectionDuplicates` runs the generator `duplicateScreen`:

- it joins items into groups with union-find;
- it skips a pair already in one group;
- it counts each comparison.

`groupsToDropped` keeps the old grouping and keep-order code unchanged.

## The yield and the token (Do step 2)

- **In the screen.** `findCollectionDuplicatesAsync` drains the same generator and awaits `defaultYieldToEventLoop`
  (a zero-delay timer) every `SCREEN_YIELD_EVERY_COMPARISONS` comparisons. That constant is the cascade's own
  `DEDUP_YIELD_EVERY_COMPARISONS`, 250. It checks the job's token on entry and after each yield.
  `screenItemsAsync` is `screenItems` with that screen.
- **In the caller.** `prepare` awaits `screenItemsAsync`. Its language loop (`detectLanguage`, about 1.8 ms a paper
  in batch G's profile) also yields every `SCREEN_LANGUAGE_YIELD_EVERY_ITEMS` (25) items and checks the token. Both
  choices were made by the coordinator, under item 313: `plan/00` §6 item 406(b) and 406(e).
- **Not changed.** `trendReportPipeline.ts` still calls the synchronous `screenItems`. It now compares only likely
  pairs too. This is recorded, not carded (item 406(c)).
