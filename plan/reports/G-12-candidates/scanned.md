SCANNED-LAYOUT SLICE: CANDIDATE LIST

I found 3 candidates and 3 reserves. None of the six has a licence statement on its PubMed Central (PMC) landing page. Each relies on a public-domain statement from the publishing agency or the hosting repository, quoted below. I downloaded no files and opened no PDF links. Every PDF path is given exactly as the landing page links it.

Before the list, two caveats apply to everything in it:

- **NIST public-domain statement.** NIST is the National Institute of Standards and Technology. Its statement covers works by NIST employees, and the National Bureau of Standards (NBS) is the earlier name of the same agency. The statement says works are "not subject to Copyright protection within the United States". For outside the United States it reserves rights, but grants a "non-exclusive, perpetual, paid-up, royalty-free, worldwide right to reprint works".
- **"Scanned" is not labelled.** No PMC page in this session says "scanned" or "PDF (scanned)". The scanned status comes from page images on the landing page, or is an expectation (pre-1990 print, large PDFs, errors from text recognition in the HTML). Someone should open each PDF to confirm.

**Shared licence sources**

- **NIST page:** https://www.nist.gov/open/copyright-fair-use-and-licensing-statements-srd-data-software-and-technical-series-publications
  - Under the heading "NIST Technical Series Publications": "This includes publishing the NIST Technical Series: technical reports and Journal of Research of NIST."
  - "Works authored by NIST employees are not subject to Copyright protection within the United States"
  - Caveat on the same page: "Some works published by NIST may have been written by third parties and may be subject to copyright protection."
- **Second NIST page:** https://www.nist.gov/node/419241 says: "Papers published in the Journal of Research of NIST are not subject to copyright in the U.S."
- **CDC Stacks** (the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention repository) item pages show the rights label "Public Domain". No other licence text appears there.
