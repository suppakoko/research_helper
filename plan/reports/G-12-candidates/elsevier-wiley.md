ELSEVIER + WILEY SLICE: 12 candidates and 4 reserves, all with a CC BY 4.0 licence confirmed on the PubMed Central (PMC) article page

How the list was built: I found articles through the Europe PMC search service and then opened each PMC article page (HTML only). I did not fetch any PDF, any supplementary file, sciencedirect.com or onlinelibrary.wiley.com. I wrote no files.
- **PDF links:** each page links its PDF with a relative href, quoted below. The full URL is that href joined to the page URL (for example https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10777411/pdf/main.pdf). I built the full URLs myself and did not fetch them.
- **Headings:** they are the top-level headings as PMC shows them. PMC may change separators, so a Wiley PDF printing "1 | INTRODUCTION" would appear on PMC as "1. INTRODUCTION". The exact form in the PDF still needs checking by eye.
- **Columns:** every column count is expected from the journal's style. None was seen.

=== NOT VERIFIED / CAVEATS ===
- **Wiley licence wording:** only the start of each licence sentence ("This is an open access article under the terms of the") and the by/4.0 link target were confirmed. The rest of the sentence was not quoted word for word by the tool.
- **E5 title:** taken from the Europe PMC record I fetched, not quoted from the PMC page itself.
- **E6 and ER1 publisher:** the PMC credit lines name the American Society for Biochemistry and Molecular Biology and AAAS, not Elsevier. Elsevier comes only from the DOI prefix.
- **Column counts:** none confirmed. Whether the Wiley PDFs print "1 |" separators, and whether supplements are appended to any main PDF, need checking when the PDFs are downloaded. Every page except W5 lists its supplements as separate files.
- **Skipped article:** the fetch tool refused one MicrobiologyOpen page (PMC9721089), so I replaced it with a different article.
- **Not reachable:** sciencedirect.com and onlinelibrary.wiley.com were not attempted.
