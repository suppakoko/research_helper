## COORDINATOR-LEVEL DECISIONS YOU TOOK

Items 1 to 6 are written in `docs/02` §4.7 as "DECIDED 2026-11-02 (coordinator, under `plan/00` §6 item 313)". Item 7 is recorded only here, as finding 1.

1. **Strategy.**
   - `"citations"` or `"references"` calls that route and lets its failure propagate. `P5-T10`'s `cachedRelated` stores whatever comes back, so an outage returned as an empty page would be remembered for 7 or 90 days.
   - `"auto"` or no strategy calls both, one after the other, with `limit` applying to each list. A failing route becomes one warning. If both fail, the citations failure is thrown.
   - Any other strategy is a `RangeError`.
2. **Seed.**
   - PMID first, then PMCID. A DOI-only seed costs one `lookup` per call.
   - A seed that resolves to neither throws `BadRequestError` with status 404, because that is what `cachedRelated` turns into a 7-day negative entry and what `docs/05` §9.2 calls "404 / unresolvable ID".
   - No HTTP 404 actually occurred in that case. The error's context carries `seedNotIndexed: true`.
3. **Paging.** `pageSize` stays constant across a call, because `page=` counts in units of `pageSize`. It is `limit` clamped to 1…1000. `nextCursor` is always absent.
4. **`rank`** is the position in the whole list, skipped rows included. A row with no `id` or `source` gets no record and no edge, and the page warns with the count.
5. **`fromDate`** is applied to the year on rows already read and sends no extra request, so a filtered call can return fewer than `limit`. Undated records are kept.
6. **Rows go through the shipped mapper** after reshaping, and `authorString` is split. `citationType` is not mapped: `type` stays `"other"` and listed in `missingFields`, except `PPR` rows, which become `"preprint"`.
7. **I edited `test/unit/sources/europepmc.test.ts` although it is not in the card's Files,** because the card's own Done-when cannot pass otherwise.
