2. **Spending order:** `RELATED_GENERATORS`, with Crossref last. Similar articles cost 2. A Europe PMC call costs 2 only when it has neither a PMID nor a PMCID.
3. **Second wave:** when a Semantic Scholar citation or reference list fails or comes back empty, the over-budget Europe PMC and Crossref generators may spend the reserve. Crossref must leave 1 request.

7. **"Could not ask" rows:** when the seed lacks the identifier, no adapter call is made, so these rows carry no adapter warning text.
8. **Seed type:** `RelatedGeneratorSeed` is `{ ids }`, so a `CanonicalWork` satisfies it.
