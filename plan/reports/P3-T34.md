How resumption is gated
- JobQueue.recover() follows §7.6 as decided:
  - running or cancelling becomes interrupted;
  - an interrupted job with attemptCount ≥ 3 becomes failed with code "resume-loop";
  - a pipeline that cannot resume becomes failed with code "not-resumable";
  - a job from another plugin version, or with no checkpoint, is left for the user (awaitingUserDecision);
  - queued and paused jobs are left alone.
- Each remaining job is resumed only when all five prerequisites exist:
  - ResumeGuards.skipCached (P3-T33),
  - ResumeGuards.egress (P3-T15, privacy.egressAcknowledged),
  - ResumeGuards.confirm (P3-T16, summary.confirmAboveCents),
  - ResumeGuards.ceilings (P3-T16, run.maxSpendCents and run.maxSessionSpendCents),
  - JobNotifier.resumed (the visible notice).
- missingResumePrerequisites() names whatever is absent. While anything is absent, the job stays `interrupted` with its attempt count unchanged. It is listed in waitingForGuards and looked at again at the next start. No guard is called.
- When all five exist, the order per job is:
  1. skipCached runs first and returns the cut-down checkpoint, so the later guards judge only the remaining work.
  2. egress, confirm and ceilings run next. The first "no" moves the job to `paused` with that guard's reason: egress-unacknowledged, awaiting-confirmation or budget-stop (pause and ask, item 234). The user gets one pause notice.
  3. JobNotifier.resumed is called once for all jobs that passed, while they are still `interrupted`. Only after it succeeds are they moved to `queued` with attemptCount + 1.
- A guard that throws, or a notice that fails, resumes nothing.
- installServices passes no guards and no notifier. P3-T15, P3-T16 and P3-T33 fill `resumeGuards` in createJobQueue's call in installServices. No card owns the notifier surface (see Contradiction 6).
- A guard must not block waiting for the user. To ask, it answers "no" with a reason.
