## Coordinator-level decisions

All are recorded as "DECIDED 2026-11-05 (coordinator, under `plan/00` §6 item 313)" in `docs/08` §5.4:

1. Heading rows appear only with more than one centroid. Groups are ordered by their best row, and rows with no topic come last.
2. A heading is the label as plain text, or "Topic n".
3. The stale label from `P5-T21` finding 5 matters: a label the user removed reads "Topic n". The groups themselves do not change.
4. Each reason is its own message on its own line.
5. `novelty` on the word space is not put on a row. The list says it once in `research-helper-search-recommend-novelty-note`. On SPECTER2 it stays on the row.
6. `similarity` names the basic kind on the word space. A row with similarity at 0, or left with nothing, reads "Found for this collection". No number is shown.
7. The preprint badge is in words in the title cell.
8. The slider is 0 to 100 in steps of 5, starting at 25. All three controls are read by Find and written nowhere. Retry repeats the failed run's values.
9. Not interested removes the row only when `reject` answers `true`. Otherwise a warning appears and the row stays. "We'll show fewer like this." is said only with a key, because nothing reads a negative without one. Focus moves to the next button, and Enter is stopped at `keydown`.
10. Show dismissed sends nothing when ticked. A hint line appears when the list came from a run that did not ask. There is no undo.
11. The picker starts on the dated new collection, nested under the source and reused the same day. Import is the preview path, and nothing is stored about it. If the source collection is gone, the import refuses.
12. A row owned by title links the item it matched, through `existingItemID`.
13. `similarity.notice` is a warning line.
14. The filter matches title, authors and year. Headings have no checkbox and no count, and an empty heading is not drawn.
