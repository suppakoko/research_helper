## Coordinator-level decisions (DECIDED, under `plan/00` §6 item 313)

**`docs/12` §15**
1. At most 5 terms per line. MeSH without check tags and arXiv categories come first, major topics first, then most frequent in the collection, then keywords.
2. The collection name is never sent. The variable holds the fixed text `not supplied`.
3. `N_PAPERS`, the year range and the histogram are counts.
4. Input budget is the smaller of 60 000 tokens and the window room, less the prompt and schema tokens. Over that, an evenly spaced subset goes. If no line fits, nothing is sent and a warning is given.
5. The cache holds only `narrative`, `suggestedQueries` and the output token count, through `redactRecord`, in the `report` namespace. The key is `membershipHash` plus library, epoch, provider, model and temperature.
6. An unusable answer falls back to "heuristic" and is not cached. A cost refusal, cancellation, refused model or transport error propagates.

**`docs/07` §5.2**
1. "No provider" means no model or no key.
2. A refused model throws before the dialog. `requiresConfirmation` forces the question.
3. The question goes through the port `ProfileCallSurface`, because `pipeline/` may not import `ui/`.
4. `profileCallOutputTokens` is the answering response's own figure, and it is cached.
5. No provider carries no `warningId`.
