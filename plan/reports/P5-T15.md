## Coordinator-level decisions (each written in `docs/08` as "DECIDED 2026-11-02 (coordinator, under `plan/00` §6 item 313)")

1. `related.enabled` has three readers: registration is skipped while it is off; the submenu's `onShowing` re-reads it; the opener reads it on every call. Skipping registration means the item menu holds no empty submenu and no separator of ours.
2. The related mode hides the keyword, Search button, year panel, result cap, databases and screening controls.
3. The related mode does not connect the keyword view model, because that application-wide object would repaint its previous run's rows under this seed. `search()` does nothing until `P5-T16`.
4. "Similar articles (PubMed)" is the fourth Include label, under `plan/08` §0.4 line 12.
5. Not drawn yet: Include counts, "Restrict to last 3 years", "Link imported items…".
6. The query carries `related: { seedItemID, relations }`, and a year list on "Any" reads as `NaN`, meaning no bound.
7. The search window now closes at shutdown in EVERY mode, including keyword. This is a behaviour change for the keyword window, needed for "leaves no window".
